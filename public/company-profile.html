About IndusVertex

IndusVertex Private Limited is a next-generation integrated engineering and infrastructure company — built to deliver what most firms cannot: complete project lifecycle ownership, from concept to commissioning, under a single roof.

We operate across power transmission, renewable energy, data centres, EV infrastructure, civil construction, environmental sustainability, automation and IT infrastructure — serving India's leading telecom majors, data centre operators, industrial corporates and government bodies. With 4000+ statutory approvals, 300+ projects delivered and operations spanning 15 domains, IndusVertex has established itself as a trusted single-window partner for complex, multi-disciplinary infrastructure challenges.

Our edge lies in in-house depth — multi-disciplinary engineering teams, regulatory and compliance expertise, and rigorous project execution — all under one point of accountability. No fragmented vendors. No coordination gaps. Just infrastructure delivered on time, on spec and in full compliance.
